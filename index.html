Ask about availability

Questions, answered.

Straight answers before you book anything.

How much does this cost?

Intro pricing is $297 for Starter, $597 for Growth and $997 for Scale, one-time and fixed. We confirm scope on a free audit before any work starts, with no hourly billing. An optional care plan is $49/month. Your GoHighLevel plan and text usage are paid to those providers directly.

How do I pay, and is it only PayPal?

No, we’re not tied to one payment method. The options and terms are confirmed on the free audit call, before any work starts.

What if it doesn’t work out?

Scope and price are locked in before work starts, so there are no surprise charges. Because the build is custom labor, there’s no blanket money-back guarantee once work is underway. If the delivered system doesn’t match what was scoped, we fix it under your included revision rounds at no extra cost. Payment and cancellation terms are settled on the free audit call, before you commit.

How fast will I start getting leads?

Results depend on your offer, traffic source and follow-up speed. The system captures, responds and books automatically, but traffic and offer quality set the volume. You should see faster response times and a better booking rate once it’s live.

Will this work for my industry?

Our deepest playbooks are for roofing, HVAC, dental and legal (appointment-based businesses where a missed call is a missed job) and for e-commerce stores, where welcome emails, order confirmations and customer follow-up drive repeat sales. The same framework works for any business that lives on fast lead response. Only the messaging and automation logic get customized.

What do you need from me to start?

Access to your GoHighLevel account, brand assets (logo and colors), offer details and your booking process. We handle the system architecture and the build.

How long does the build take?

Starter: 3–5 days. Growth: 7–10 days. Scale: 10–14 days. It depends on scope and revisions.

What happens after I say yes?

If you have an existing workflow, we start with a full audit of your funnel, pipeline, tags and automation logic to find gaps, then rebuild. Starting from scratch, we map the customer journey and system architecture first, then build in stages with full end-to-end testing before launch.
